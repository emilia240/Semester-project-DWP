# 🌿 To Be Human

A photo-sharing platform for **human-made arts and crafts**: no ads, no AI images, just people making things.

This is a beginner project, made while learning PHP and databases, and it is still a work in progress.

## The idea

Most photo apps are built around ads, algorithms and endless scrolling. To Be Human is meant to feel calmer: a place for people who make things by hand, like ceramics, textiles, woodwork, painting, jewellery and knitting, to show their work and see what others are making.

## What it is about

- Sharing photos of handmade work, with a short story about how it was made
- Following other makers and finding new ones by craft
- Liking, commenting and saving favourite posts
- Taking part in friendly community challenges
- A safe, welcoming space with simple rules and a way to report anything that does not belong

## How it is built

The code is split into small, readable parts (models, views and controllers) with one entry point, so it is easy to follow while learning. Security is part of the plan from the start: prepared statements for the database, escaped output, hashed passwords and protected forms.

**Built with:** PHP, MySQL/MariaDB, Tailwind CSS and daisyUI.
